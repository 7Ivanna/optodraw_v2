A cross-section cuts a GDS layout along a straight line and runs the process its layer stack implies: the
etches that leave the rib and slab, the implants, the contacts etched through the oxide and filled, the metals
and the passivation. The walls slope as the stack says. One cut gives each look of the same regions.

## The device

The PN rib phase shifter of the GDSFactory generic PDK, `straight_pn` with its default `via_stack_slab_m3`,
as GDS records, on the bundled `gdsfactory_generic` stack. The contacts climb through M1 and M2 to M3,
the pad metal. The oxide is one body over M3, and PADOPEN (46/0) etches it down to the pads. The stack has
no NP and PP, so the cell leaves them out. For a file, pass its path: `cross_section("chip.gds", ...)`.

## Cut 3D slab

<!-- cell: cross-sections/03-slab -->

A block of the layout behind the cut. Its face is the cross-section above. Behind it the process runs again
wherever the layout changes, so a contact shows only where it is drawn. Toward the back the oxide is cut
away in steps, with the wiring above each step: first M1 on its oxide, then the bare rib and its implants.

```python
xs.slab("pn_slab.glb")         # for Blender or any glTF viewer
xs.slab("pn_slab.png")         # the viewer's image, headless: needs Chrome, Chromium or Edge
xs.slab("pn_slab.html")        # the interactive view as one page
```

## Top view

<!-- cell: cross-sections/04-top -->

The same process runs on a cut along each pixel row and column. `stage="chip"` is the finished chip: a
device under the oxide shows faintly (`see_through`, in µm), and metal hides what lies under it.
`stage="device"` stops before the first oxide. `tilt` and `rotation` set the view in degrees. Turned to the
layout's edges, every cut is new: a minute at 1000 px.

## In the editor

Select a GDS part. In the cross-section panel, cut along the Layout view's ruler (M, two clicks, Cut along
ruler) or scan a vertical cut, then pick a look. The cut uses the part's own stack.

## Check it

`xs.check()` names a drawn layer the stack does not know, an implant on etched silicon and a contact that
lands on nothing. The first cell asserts it is empty.
